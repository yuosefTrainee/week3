Cute Animals

Cats are small, playful, and independent animals. They are popular pets around the world.

Dogs are friendly and loyal animals. They are often kept as pets and can be trained easily.

Lions are large wild cats that live mainly in Africa. They are known for living in groups called prides.

Tigers are large wild cats known for their orange fur and black stripes. They are powerful hunters.

Horses are strong animals that have been used by humans for transportation and work for thousands of years.
